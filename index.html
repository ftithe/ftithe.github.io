Publications

1. M. Asrat. "An integrable deformation of the sine(sinh)-Gordon model – Non-Lie algebra." JHEP 09 (2026) 208. [Paper Link]

Summary: The paper presents a new classically integrable model in two dimensions. It is a deformation of the sine(sinh)-Gordon model. It describes classical string theory in ${\cal A}_3$ [1]. The model is based on a non-Lie Malcev-like algebra (Malcev-like because the basis satisfies the Malcev identity), or more precisely a special non-Lie alternative Akivis algebra, rather than a conventional Lie algebra. I obtained the Lax pair and r-matrix of the model. The r-matrix is non-dynamical and it satisfies the classical Yang–Baxter equation. Therefore, smooth functions or observables on the phase space equipped with the Poisson bracket form a Lie algebra. That is, for complete integrability the underlying symmetry algebra need not be a Lie algebra. This is the key and interesting result. I showed the existence of infinitely many conserved quantities that are in involution. Thus, I proved that the model is a classical integrable field theory. This extends the algebraic foundation of integrability beyond Lie algebra. I also obtained a family of integrable models based on finite dimensional non-Lie alternative Akivis algebras. They are related to the Poisson-Boltzmann equation in two dimensions.
